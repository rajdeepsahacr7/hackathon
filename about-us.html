The Origin Story

The Coded Cipher exists to prove that curiosity, not credentials, solves the hardest problems. What began as a handful of engineers meeting after hours to trade half-finished side projects has grown into a full investigation — a hackathon built for builders who'd rather ask "what if" than wait for permission.

Every case we open follows the same method Holmes himself would recognise: gather the evidence, question the obvious, and build something that couldn't have existed an hour earlier. Forty-eight hours, one deadline, and a room full of people convinced the impossible is just a puzzle they haven't solved yet.
